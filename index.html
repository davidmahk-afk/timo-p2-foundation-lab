How Cyrus should use this

One skill at a time. Explore the idea first, then see how the same mathematics can wear different “story clothes”. Mark a skill only when Cyrus can explain why the method works.

1 · Learn

Understand the rule or structure in plain language.

先明白規律或結構，不急着計答案。

2 · Explore

Move sliders, tap objects and change the numbers to see what remains true.

自己改數字、拖動控制，看看哪些關係仍然不變。

3 · Same idea

Compare different stories that are secretly the same problem.

把題目故事拿走，找出背後相同的數學結構。

4 · Explain

Say the idea aloud before ticking “I can explain it”.

能夠用自己的說話解釋「為甚麼」，才算真正掌握。

Six concept families

The papers use five official headings, but these six learning families make the repeating mathematical ideas easier to see.

Patterns & rules

Changing jumps, alternating tracks, rule machines and arithmetic sequences.

Relationships & cycles

Age gaps, weekdays, queues and counting gaps rather than objects.

Arithmetic structure

Friendly pairs, regrouping factors, undoing operations and carrying.

Number structure

Multiples, divisibility, odd/even, packing and remainders.

Systematic geometry

Segments, rectangles, corners, pyramids and cube views.

Counting & optimisation

Routes, place value, largest/smallest constructions and maximum buying power.
